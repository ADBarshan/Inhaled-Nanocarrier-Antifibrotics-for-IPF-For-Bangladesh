Development and Local Implementation of Inhaled Nanocarrier Antifibrotics for DPLD: A Low-Cost Aerosol Delivery Framework for Bangladesh

Project Rationale & Objectives Standard oral therapies for DPLD rely on systemic delivery, requiring large daily doses (e.g., 2,403 mg pirfenidone or 300 mg nintedanib daily) that lead to severe gastrointestinal distress and hepatotoxicity, requiring costly blood monitoring. Transitioning existing APIs to inhaled nanocarrier or aerosol platforms focuses drug mass directly into distal alveolar tissue.

Primary Objective: Formulate and validate inhaled delivery platforms (Solid Lipid Nanoparticles, PLGA micro-agglomerates, or DPIs) for established antifibrotic APIs.

Cost Target: Reduce annual drug expenditure in Bangladesh from ৳135,000–৳252,000 BDT down to ৳18,000–৳42,000 BDT.

Safety Target: Eliminate systemic hepatotoxicity and cut annual diagnostic monitoring expenses by over 75%.
